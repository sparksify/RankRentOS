# Batch 1 — First 10 Rank & Rent Assets
### Production Discovery run · 2026-08-04 · Lens `rr-opportunity-v1.0` · weights `w1.0` · qualification `qual-v1.0`

## How this list was produced

1. **Universe**: 1,392 markets (24 niches × 58 cities) from the v0 corpus, with DataForSEO volume/CPC observed 2026-08-03 (fresh, ≤90-day policy).
2. **Low-cost qualification** (free): 258 passed · 170 need research · 964 rejected — every verdict stored with reasons.
3. **Deep research** on a diversified queue of 30 candidates (max 3 per niche): current web-search competitive evidence (30 SERP snapshots, ~230 businesses resolved, 141 operator candidates), 269 domain candidates verified **registrar-grade via Vercel on 2026-08-04**.
4. **Opportunity Lens v1** scored all 30; portfolio selection applied confidence ≥0.65, verified-domain-required, ≤3 per niche, ≤4 per region.

**Honesty note on confidence (0.76–0.78 across the batch):** SERP evidence this cycle is *web-search proxy* — real, current results, but not localized Google SERPs with local-pack/review data. That caps confidence below ~0.80 by design. Every score lands in the 70–79 "**build with caveats — verify first**" band, not the 80+ "strong build" band. The uniform recommended next action: run one localized SERP check (SerpAPI/DataForSEO, ~$0.05/market) per target before registering domains. Domains are $11.25 each and verified available *today* — availability decays, so verify-and-register should happen together.

## Why each target made the cut

1. **Mold Remediation — Frisco, TX (78.5).** Highest overall score. 170/mo measured volume at $27.98 CPC (very expensive clicks = valuable leads). Top-3 organic is Yelp + a SERVPRO-of-Allen service page + Thumbtack — **no Frisco-based remediator ranks**. A rank-and-rent-style site (waterdamagefrisco.org) already ranks at #4, proving the model works in this exact market. `friscomold.com` is a rare short EMD. Risk: medium PE roll-up pressure in restoration.
2. **Dumpster Rental — Frisco, TX (75.0).** 480/mo measured volume at $11.66 CPC — the strongest demand in the batch. Positions 1–4 are directories/franchise/national broker; Yelp's own snippet exposed a tail of small local haulers (Maverick, Grouchy's, DFW Easy Dumpsters) with zero organic presence — a deep lead-buyer pool. Need-type, non-seasonal, fast-repeat niche.
3. **Auto Glass — McKinney, TX (73.0).** 390/mo at $10.49 CPC. Safelite holds the top spots, but an exact-match local domain already ranks #5 — dedicated local sites demonstrably rank here, and 6 independent shops (the operator bench) sit below it. Insurance-funded need purchase.
4. **Land Clearing — Conroe, TX (73.0).** 140/mo at a striking $44.61 CPC. Yelp holds #1–2 and the top local operator (A&E Clearing) ranks *only* through Yelp with no site of its own. High-ticket jobs ($3k–20k) in the Houston growth corridor; best revenue range of the batch ($720–1,200/mo).
5. **Water Softeners — Apex, NC (73.0).** The #1 preliminary-score market confirmed: top 3 organic are all directories (Yelp/BBB/Angi), no dedicated local softener site exists, and `apexwatersofteners.com` is open. 50/mo measured at $6.91 CPC, $130k median income, high-growth Raleigh suburb. Geographic learning: our only NC probe.
6. **Dumpster Rental — Georgetown, TX (71.5).** 260/mo at $10.86 CPC and **no genuinely Georgetown-based company ranks anywhere** — every competitor reaches the city via out-of-area service pages. Stamps the dumpster playbook into the Austin metro (one-niche-many-cities leverage with #2).
7. **Air Duct Cleaning — Gilbert, AZ (70.5).** 110/mo at $12.57 CPC. The strongest directory wall observed in the batch: positions 1–4 are all directories and no competitor has a Gilbert-dedicated site. 90% margin niche; pairs with target #10 for a Gilbert operator cluster.
8. **Air Duct Cleaning — Parker, CO (70.5).** 260/mo at $9.89 CPC. The city-named incumbent ("Parker Duct Cleaning") ranks only via its Facebook page — no website of its own on page one; it is simultaneously the SERP gap and the most natural lead buyer. Niche repeat with #7.
9. **Appliance Repair — Forney, TX (70.0).** 140/mo at $7.74 CPC. Top 6 results are all directories/nationals/franchises; the #1 result is a *Yelp profile* for a business with no visible site. Locals only appear at positions 7–9. Highest-volume niche family in the corpus (validated 50 cities wide) — cheap learning for a repeatable play.
10. **Water Softeners — Gilbert, AZ (70.0).** 140/mo at $26.33 CPC (expensive clicks). Fragmented SERP with no franchise presence; the only exact-match site ranks *last* while its Facebook page outranks it. Niche repeat with #5 and city cluster with #7.

## Portfolio logic (why not just the top-10 raw scores)

The raw ranking and recommended batch coincide this cycle because the concentration guards (≤3/niche, ≤4/region) were satisfied naturally by the diversified deep-research queue. The batch still encodes deliberate leverage:
- **One-niche-many-cities**: dumpster rental ×2 (Frisco/Georgetown), air duct ×2 (Gilbert/Parker), water softeners ×2 (Apex/Gilbert) — three playbooks each tested in two markets.
- **One-operator-many-assets / geographic clustering**: DFW-north cluster (#1, #2, #3, #9 all within ~30 min), Gilbert AZ pair (#7, #10).
- **Learning spread**: 7 niches, 6 metros, 4 states; mix of need (mold, auto glass, appliance, dumpster, duct) and affluent-desire (softeners) purchases; one high-ticket commission-capable play (land clearing).
